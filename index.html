What Is Fat FIRE?

Fat FIRE is a subset of the financial independence, retire early (FIRE) movement focused on accumulating enough wealth to maintain a high standard of living after retirement. Unlike lean FIRE, which emphasizes extreme frugality, Fat FIRE targets a post-retirement annual spending of $100,000 or more, typically requiring a portfolio of $2.5 million and up.

The philosophy is straightforward: earn more, invest strategically, and build a life where early retirement doesn't mean downgrading your lifestyle.

Core Principles of Fat FIRE

  • Income acceleration - Prioritize career moves, side businesses, and equity compensation that dramatically increase earnings
  • Tax-efficient investing - Maximize tax-advantaged accounts, utilize tax-loss harvesting, and structure investments to minimize drag
  • Asset allocation - Diversify across index funds, real estate, and alternative investments based on your timeline and risk tolerance
  • Lifestyle design - Spend intentionally on what matters to you while cutting costs on things that don't move the needle
  • Withdrawal strategy - Plan for decades of spending using dynamic withdrawal rates, Roth conversion ladders, and multiple income streams

How Much Do You Need?

The standard Fat FIRE target depends on your desired annual spending. Using the 4% rule as a baseline (adjusted for conservatism to 3.5% for longer retirements), here are common benchmarks:

  • $100K/year spending = $2.85M portfolio
  • $150K/year spending = $4.3M portfolio
  • $200K/year spending = $5.7M portfolio
  • $300K/year spending = $8.6M portfolio

These numbers shift based on factors like geographic cost of living, healthcare costs before Medicare eligibility, family size, and whether you plan to maintain any income streams in retirement.

Building Your Fat FIRE Plan

A successful Fat FIRE journey starts with clarity on three numbers: your current savings rate, your target number, and your timeline. From there, the work is in optimizing income, investment returns, and tax efficiency across every available lever.

High-income professionals in tech, medicine, finance, and law are particularly well-positioned for Fat FIRE, but the principles apply to any high earner willing to be strategic about their financial trajectory.
