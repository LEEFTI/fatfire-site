What Is Fat FIRE?

Fat FIRE is a subset of the financial independence, retire early (FIRE) movement for people who want to retire early without living on a shoestring budget. While lean FIRE targets $30-40K per year in retirement spending, Fat FIRE aims for $100K or more, requiring a portfolio of $2.5M and up.

The philosophy centers on earning more and investing strategically rather than extreme frugality. It's for high-income professionals in tech, medicine, finance, law, and business who want freedom without compromise.
